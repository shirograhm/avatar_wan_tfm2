Adds Avatar Wan, the first Avatar, as a fully playable ranged champion to Teamfight Manager 2.

Wan fights by cycling through the four elements. Every element changes what his basic attacks do, so his damage, sustain, and speed all come from which element he is standing in when the fight starts — and from how well he rotates through them mid-fight.

[b]Wan comes with his own sprite, skill icons, projectiles, and per-element effects. No extra setup required — just enable the mod.[/b]

[h1] Kit [/h1]

[b]Soul of Raava[/b] (Basic Attack)
Wan's basic attacks deal 50% AD physical damage and 50% AD magic damage, and apply a different on-hit effect based on his current element.

[b]The Avatar Cycle[/b] (Skill 1)
Wan swaps to the next element in-sequence.
- [b]Fire:[/b] Burn for 12 (+30% AP) magic damage over 3 seconds.
- [b]Air:[/b] Gain 6% movement speed for 4 seconds (max 4 stacks).
- [b]Water:[/b] Heal for 3 + 3% of your missing health.
- [b]Earth:[/b] Basic attacks deal 40% of their damage to nearby enemies.

[b]Spirit Step[/b] (Skill 2)
Wan dashes a short distance in any direction. For the next 3 seconds, he stores 80% of his damage taken and gains 10% (+0.03% AD) bonus attack speed. When Spirit Step expires, Wan heals himself for 30 + 80% of the stored damage.

[b]Harmonic Convergence[/b] (Ultimate)
Wan channels Raava's full power, gaining a 300 (+60% AP) (+6% HP) health shield for 6 seconds. For the duration, Wan's basic attacks apply the effects of all 4 elements. Harmonic Convergence's duration is extended by 1.5 seconds whenever Wan gets a takedown.

[h1] How He Plays [/h1]

Wan is a ranged AD/AP hybrid who wants both damage stats — half of every auto is physical and half is magic, so pure armor or pure magic resist stacking never fully answers him.

The element you leave him on matters. Fire is the raw damage pattern, Earth is the wave-clear and teamfight pattern, Water is the sustain pattern, and Air is the kiting pattern. He spawns on Fire and cycles forward from there.

Spirit Step is both his escape and his heal — the more damage he eats during the window, the bigger the heal when it ends. Dashing in and taking a hit on purpose is often correct.

Harmonic Convergence is the payoff: for its duration he is not choosing an element, he is applying all four on every single auto, and takedowns keep it alive.

[h1] Important [/h1]

This mod currently supports the English locale only. You can use it with other languages, but Wan's name and skill descriptions will be broken.

If you would like to provide translations, feel free to shoot me a message on Discord @shirograhm.

Saves played with this mod enabled may break if you later load the save with the mod disabled, since Wan will no longer exist on any roster that drafted him.

Currently updated for game version 0.5.5.

[h1] Instructions [/h1]

1. Subscribe and enable the mod in the game's mod menu.
2. Start a new save (recommended) or load an existing one.
3. Wan will show up in the champion pool for drafting like any other champion.

If Wan does not appear, that usually means the save is not loading the mod order. To fix this:

1. Save your current game and go back to the main menu.
2. Click Load -> Load on the save you just made. It will probably say "No Info" under mods.
3. Once launched, progress once and save again. Then go back to the main menu.
4. Click Load -> Load again, this time the mods column should read "Match". <-- (That means it's loading correctly)
5. Play as you would normally!

[h1] Known Issues [/h1]

Some effects are roughly simulated to the best of my ability using the available mod-sdk.

The AI does not always understand when to rotate elements, so it may sit on a suboptimal element longer than a human player would.

Balance numbers are still being tuned. Feedback is very welcome.
